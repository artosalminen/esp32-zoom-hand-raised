What to check

  1. Join outside meeting with ZAK: the join succeeds for a meeting hosted on another Zoom account. If it fails, the log shows Zoom's reason.
  2. No co-host needed: with this console not co-host, hands raised by others appear in the table.
  3. Within 2 s: "Δ since mark" on detections stays under 2 s, for both raising and lowering, and for "Lower all hands".
  4. Own row: exactly one row is marked "me".
  5. No Marketplace approval: points 1–4 work with the app unpublished.
